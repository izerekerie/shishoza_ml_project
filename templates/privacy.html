Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated 22 July 2026

Land certificates are never stored

When you upload a land title to identify your parcel, the file is read in memory only to extract the location and outline. It is not saved to disk, and owner names and national ID numbers on the certificate are never kept. Crop out your name before uploading if you prefer.

What we do keep

If you create a citizen account, we store your email, a securely hashed password, and (optionally) your name and phone so we can route your review requests and email you the manager's decision. When you send a parcel for technical review, we save the analysed parcel's location, risk results, your stated reason for cutting, and any site photo and caption you choose to attach.

Photos you attach

A site photo is optional. If you add one, it is downscaled in your browser before it is sent and is shown only to the district forest manager handling your request (and to you, on your "My reviews" page). Don't include faces, documents, or anything you would not want a reviewer to see.

Satellite data

Risk assessments are built from public satellite datasets (Sentinel-2, Sentinel-1, SRTM and Hansen Global Forest Change). They describe the area around your land, not your exact plot, and are not a live reading.

Your choices

You can view your submitted requests any time on your "My reviews" page. To have your account or a request removed, contact your district forest office.

Anonymous lookups

You can check a parcel without creating an account. Anonymous requests carry no user identity, and your IP address is never stored in readable form — it is converted to an irreversible hash. Your lookups cannot be traced back to you.

Terms of Use

How the risk result is produced

The HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW result comes from a machine-learning model and transparent rules. It is a screening signal, not an official decision. A forest officer, not the software, makes the permit decision, and you can ask why a parcel was flagged.

Accuracy and limits

The model can miss small clearings or over-flag a parcel, and results reflect the available satellite imagery. Treat the result as guidance, not proof.

Forest-manager accounts

Authenticated managers can access only parcels in their assigned district. Land data is not open to any logged-in user.

Your rights and contact

You may request information about how the service handles your data, and ask to access, correct, or delete it, consistent with Rwanda's Law Nº 058/2021 on the protection of personal data. To do so, contact your district forest office.

Note

Shishoza is a research project supporting sustainable forest management in Rwanda. A technical review here supports the official permit decision — it does not replace it.
